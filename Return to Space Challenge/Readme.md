# 🚀 Return To Space Challenge — Power BI Project  
A Maven Analytics challenge focused on exploring space missions data and answering three major space questions through data visualization.  
This dashboard analyzes global rocket launches, mission outcomes, and organizational performance to uncover the true champions of space exploration.

---

## 📌 Project Objective  
To design a **single Power BI report** consisting of **three interactive pages** that answer:

1. **Golden Era of Space** – Which decade had the most launches and highest success rate?  
2. **Ultimate Rocket Ranking** – Which rockets dominated space history based on launch count and reliability?  
3. **Winning Team** – Which countries and organizations led the space race in launches and successful missions?

The goal was to transform raw CSV data into meaningful insights while maintaining a visually appealing and intuitive dashboard.

---

## 🛰️ Dataset Overview  
The dataset includes:  
- **4,630+ space missions** from 1950s–2020s  
- Rocket details (name, status, cost)  
- Country & company launching the mission  
- Date, location, mission status  
- Success/Failure outcomes  

Key columns used:  
`Rocket`, `Company`, `Country`, `MissionStatus`, `Price`, `Date`, `RocketStatus`

---

## 📁 Pages Overview & Insights  

### 🔵 **1. Golden Era of Space**
This page highlights decade-wise launches, successful missions, and success rate.

**Key Insights:**
- **1970s** recorded the **highest launches (1012)**.  
- **1980s** achieved the **best mission success rate (93.66%)**.  
- Overall success rate across decades: **89.89%**.  
- Launch activity gradually declines after 1980s, but efficiency improves.

---

### 🔵 **2. Ultimate Rocket Ranking**
Focuses on identifying top-performing rockets based on launches and reliability.

**Key Insights:**
- **Cosmos-3M (11K65M)** is the **ultimate champion rocket** with **446 launches**.  
- Majority of rockets fall under **10–100 launches**, indicating short lifecycles.  
- Rockets with high launches also show high success rates, proving reliability improves with time.  
- Price data was cleaned to evaluate total dollars spent.

---

### 🔵 **3. Winning Team**
Analyzes leaders across countries and organizations.

**Key Insights:**
- **USA** leads in total launches: **1467**  
- **Russia** leads in successful missions: **1323**  
- **RVSN USSR** dominates both launches (**1777**) and successes (**1614**) among companies.  
- Over **22 countries** and **62 companies** contributed to space missions.
