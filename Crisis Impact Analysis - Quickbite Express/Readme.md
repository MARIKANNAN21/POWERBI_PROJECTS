# 🍽️ QuickBite Express – Crisis Recovery Power BI Dashboard  

### 📊 End-to-End Business Analysis | Power BI | DAX | 2025  

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement  

QuickBite Express, a Bengaluru-based food-tech startup, faced a severe business crisis in June 2025 caused by:  
- Viral social media backlash over food safety.  
- Week-long delivery outages during the monsoon.  
- Competitor takeover campaigns.  

📉 **Impact:**  
- Customer churn surged to 70%.  
- Order volume dropped by 68%.  
- Average ratings fell from 4.5⭐ to 2.6⭐.  
- Partner restaurants became inactive.  

The goal: **Use data analytics to identify recovery opportunities, rebuild trust, and optimize delivery operations.**

## 👥 Customer Segmentation  

![Customer Segmentation](Images/Customer%20Segments.png)  

**Key Insights:**  
- **Active Customers:** 93% (100K of 108K total).  
- **Retention Rate:** 14.1%; **Churn Rate:** 69.6%.  
- **Customer Lifetime Decline:** 70K users dropped after crisis.  
- **Top Drop Segments:** North Indian cuisine and Bengaluru users (19.7K).  
- **Loyal Base:** 26 high-rated, high-frequency users identified for advocacy.  

📈 **Trend:** Major customer loss between May–July; stability improved by August.  

---

## 🍔 Order Patterns  

![Order Patterns](Images/Order%20Patterns.png)  

**Key Insights:**  
- **Total Orders:** 149K; **Order Drop:** 68.9%.  
- **Pre-crisis Orders:** 114K → fell to **35K during crisis**.  
- **City-wise Drop:** Highest in Bengaluru (70.2%) and Chennai (68.1%).  
- **Top Performing Restaurants:** Punjabi Express Central, Hot & Crispy Biryani Heaven.  
- **Category Trend:** Indian & Fast Food remained strong in recovery.  

📊 **Conclusion:** Clear demand recovery seen post-crisis but volumes still below pre-crisis peak.  

---

## 🛵 Delivery Performance  

![Delivery Performance](Images/Delivery%20Performance.png)  

**Key Insights:**  
- **Average Delivery Time:** 44.4 mins.  
- **SLA Compliance:** Dropped from 43.2% → 11.8% during crisis.  
- **On-Time Deliveries:** Only 36.1%.  
- **Cancellation Rate:** 7.45%.  
- **City Hotspots:** Bengaluru & Mumbai had most delays.  

⚡ **Top Insight:**  
In-house partners achieved better SLA than third-party. Operational issues during June–July caused major reputation damage.  

---

## 🍴 Restaurant Partnership  

![Restaurant Partnership](Images/Restaurant%20Partnership.png)  

**Key Insights:**  
- **Active Restaurants:** 18K of 20K (90%).  
- **Inactive:** 2K due to low SLA or quality issues.  
- **Cuisine-wise SLA:** Avg 36%; Fast Food & South Indian led performance.  
- **Top Earning Restaurants:** Punjabi Express Central (₹37K), Thindi Mane Pizza Café (₹33K).  
- **5⭐ Restaurants:** Annapurna & Bombay Darshini group — consistent service excellence.  

🏁 **Conclusion:** Partner quality improved; re-engagement campaigns boosted restaurant retention from 75% → 92%.  

---

## 💬 Reviews & Sentiment  

![Reviews Dashboard](Images/Reviews.png)  

**Key Insights:**  
- **Positive Ratings:** 58.8%; **Negative:** 18.3%.  
- **Average Rating Trend:** 4.6⭐ → 2.6⭐ → 4.1⭐ (steady recovery).  
- **City Insight:** Chennai & Delhi showed lowest post-crisis ratings.  
- **Cuisine Insight:** Fast Food and Biryani had lowest average ratings.  
- **Review Keywords:** “Late”, “Cold food”, “Packaging issue” — frequent in negative feedback.  

💡 **Observation:** Strong correlation between SLA and Rating recovery — faster deliveries = higher sentiment score.
